# Frozen disjoint program-pattern evaluation

This evaluation was designed after primary r5301/r5302 and fixed-dose r5401 results were known, before any query on its new data. It is a new assessment of the same six inherited fitted sources, not six new training seeds. No model is fitted, selected, or changed. The primary and fixed-dose registrations and results remain intact.

The original four-symbol bank used q+q for permutations q. The original five-symbol bank relabelled only BACDBAEC and BDECBADE. All length-eight words in either original pattern orbit are excluded here, even after arbitrary symbol relabelling. New words use all four/five named symbols, and adjacent symbols are unequal, matching the numerical training domain. This evaluates word-pattern generalization at length eight; it does not test extrapolation in length.

Four-symbol members are independently uniform over the 888 remaining words that each cover all four triples. Five-symbol first members are uniform over the 25,680 remaining words that admit a partner; second members are uniform over all remaining words whose active-triple union with the first covers all ten triples. Candidate bucket sizes weight the second draw. This sequential law is not uniform over all valid pairs. The same six named cells are assessed: ABCD, ABDE, ACEF, ABCDE, ACDEG, BCDEF.

For each case, 128 independent clusters draw their twelve programs, then a shared root uniformly conditional on every old/A/B reference current state and primitive-half input avoiding the fixed union of numerical training and component-test inputs. Evaluation states never enlarge the excluded set. Repeats between independent clusters are allowed. All six banks, code, seeds, and criteria are frozen before new queries. Independent interpreters check all labels and exclusion conditions; data generation makes zero model queries.

Seven endpoints are copied by exact weight identity from each corresponding fixed-dose case: source, two, three, exchange1, clean1, exchange2, clean2. An inherited scientific/raw dependency failure is retained as no completed evaluation, never a zero score or replacement source. A single look at n=128 is used. Simultaneous two-sided Clopper–Pearson bounds cover all 252 binary strict paired success probabilities with total error .025. One-sided Hoeffding bounds for 48 paired differences in [-1,1] use total error .025. Thus this assessment has its own simultaneous 95% guarantee, conditional on the fitted models and generator. Families from different assessments are not combined into a single 95% claim, and no inference over a training-seed population is asserted.

High expected outcomes require lower bounds >=.80; low outcomes require upper bounds <=.20. Source/two are low for all six cells; three/clean1/clean2 are high. Exchange predictions use exactly the original A/B changed-face sets: a cell is high iff its complete triple boundary avoids the changed set. All six three-minus-two gains and both within-endpoint signed ABCD/ABDE crossovers require lower bounds >.30. All six cases must pass; every failure is reported. There is no threshold adaptation, repeated look, extra fitting, or reselection based on this evaluation. This evaluation does not re-estimate topology or replace the original cell qualification bands.

Raw policy probabilities, numeric token IDs, states, termination and all paired scores are reconstructed. Parent weight archives and their identities remain part of the reproducibility package. All new raw outputs are archived and copied to the two existing local destinations. The cloud server remains on under the user's current instruction.
